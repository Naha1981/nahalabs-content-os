# NahaLabs Reactivate v0.31 — CRM & Opportunity Pipeline

## Goal
Turn commercial events into a persistent opportunity pipeline with stages, deal value, follow-ups and conversion history.

## Scope
- Opportunity CRUD and stage history.
- Stages: Discovery, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost.
- ZAR deal values and pipeline/won totals.
- Prospect and campaign linkage.
- CRM summary and pipeline UI.
- No automated sales actions.

## Integrity
No revenue or conversion is inferred. Opportunities are explicit CRM records entered or updated by a human/workflow.
