# NahaLabs Reactivate v0.33 — Autonomous Daily Operator

## Goal
Provide one deterministic daily work queue from persisted Reactivate data.

## Actions
- due prospect follow-ups
- first outreach for qualified HOT/HIGH prospects with no completed outreach
- missing next actions on qualified prospects
- blocked campaigns
- campaigns awaiting human approval
- campaigns ready to publish
- campaigns awaiting measurement
- due CRM opportunity follow-ups
- queued/scheduled publishing jobs

## Safety
No sending, publishing, or commercial claims happen automatically. The operator only surfaces actions supported by stored data. Invalid dates are ignored; disqualified prospects are excluded.
