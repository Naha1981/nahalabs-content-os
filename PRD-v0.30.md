# Reactivate v0.30 — Revenue & Conversion Intelligence

## Goal
Connect outreach, campaigns and performance to observable commercial outcomes without inventing attribution.

## Scope
- Persist revenue/conversion events per prospect and optional campaign.
- Support LEAD, MEETING, OPPORTUNITY, WON, LOST and REVENUE.
- Store amount/currency/source/notes/timestamps.
- Expose prospect revenue summaries via API.
- Surface leads, meetings, wins and commercial value in Command Center.
- Keep attribution observational; no unsupported causal claims.

## Non-goals
- Automated CRM sync.
- Payment processing.
- Guaranteed attribution or ROI claims.
