# NahaLabs Reactivate v0.17 — AI Content Generation Engine

## Goal
Convert an evidence-backed prospect into reviewable production-ready content assets.

## Flow
Prospect + Social Gap + verified evidence + PatternVault → content assets → human review → production.

## Guardrails
- No invented business facts.
- Unverified Social Gap produces DRAFT content and prohibits unsupported quiet-period claims.
- PatternVault supplies structural inspiration, not copied text.
- No automatic publishing.
- AI provider remains swappable; v0.17 uses a deterministic generation engine until an LLM provider is configured.

## Asset types
Reel script, FAQ post, reactivation post.
